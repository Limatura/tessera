`tess ui` makes the site's other two kinds of share: Ctrl-N (`n` with the vim keys) writes a note, with a title and a language for its highlighting taken from the title's extension, or makes a link that sends its visitors on. Enter on one of your shares shows it in full: kind, size, when it was made and expires, downloads so far and left, whether it is sealed or asks a password, its link and a QR code. Folders can be ordered newest or largest first (F6, `o` with the vim keys, or the label beside the path), saved with the other settings; the settings' Account row now does what it says. When the instance cannot be reached, the header says so and F5 tries again. `tess ls` names a link share `(link)` instead of `(note)`.
