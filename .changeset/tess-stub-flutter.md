In the full-screen view, sending looks more like printing a ticket. While a transfer runs, a ticket machine prints the ticket as far as the upload has got. When it ends, the QR code prints from the top under a print head, also when the terminal draws it as an image. The stub then hangs on through a few gusts of autumn wind, comes loose, and turns over as it falls to the ground among the leaves. The footer shows a small progress bar instead of a moving block, and signing in waits with a spinner.
