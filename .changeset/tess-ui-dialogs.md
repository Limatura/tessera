`tess ui`: the buttons of a dialog take the arrow keys and Tab, and a question to delete or stop starts on the harmless answer. In the send form Ctrl-S sends from any field and the arrows go across Send and Cancel. A drag that leaves the window is still copied. F3 opens settings (keys, mouse, dotfiles, colour), saved to the config file as they change.
