In the full-screen view, what is inside a marked folder shows as marked, and unmarking something there leaves it out of the share while the rest of the folder keeps its place. The send form counts a password that a key comes from up to the 12 characters it needs, and says so before sending rather than after. The QR codes are framed by a narrower margin, and `tess login` shows one even when it opens a browser.
