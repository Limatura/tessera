`tess ui` sends and downloads in the background: the view stays usable while they run, several can run at once, the footer shows how far the oldest has got, and F4 lists them all with their progress and a way to stop each. When one ends, its link (or what went wrong) shows as soon as the dialog in front is closed, and only the files it sent are unmarked.
