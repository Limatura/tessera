In the full-screen view, a QR code sits beside a share's details when the window is wide enough, under them when it is tall enough, and is left out with a word when neither, instead of being cut off. It is printed in the ticket's paper and ink, and with the lowest error correction, so it comes out smaller. Esc and Backspace close a share's details, which neither did, and the other dialogs. A share sealed with a password no longer says it opens only with its link.
